# Product Vision

Masyarakat helps Islamic organisations introduce themselves publicly, become discoverable, publish information, coordinate internal work and schedules, and record financial sources and usage with governance. The differentiator is public presence + information hub + operations + financial governance in one platform.

For community users the bulletin is the main attraction: it is what brings them back each day (ADR-008). Public presence and discovery exist to lead people to an organisation's bulletin; operations and finance stay inside the management workspace.
